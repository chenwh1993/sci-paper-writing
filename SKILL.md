# `sci-paper-writing` — Full-Manuscript CNS Scientific Writing & Narrative Architect Skill

An end-to-end, empirically grounded scientific writing system uniting **strategic storyline architecture** (how top-tier authors reverse-engineer Title, Abstract, Introduction, Subheadings, Discussion, and Cover Letter from a given set of Results) with **authentic CNS grammar, tense discipline, verb certainty ladders, transition corpora, Figure Captions, Methods/STAR Methods, and automated pre-submission auditing**.

Empirically distilled and cross-validated across **2020–2026**:
- **6,665** high-impact tumor-omics titles (`2,725` from 2025–2026 + `3,940` from 2020–2024) & **1,960** full-text manuscripts (`1,464` 2025–2026 derivation + `496` 2020–2024 out-of-time hold-out validation across *Nature*, *Cell*, *Science*, *Cancer Cell*, *Nat Med*, *Nat Cancer*, *Nat Genet*, *Nat Immunol*, *Nat Methods*, *Immunity*, *Sci Transl Med*, *Cancer Discov*, *Cell Rep Med*, *Nat Commun*)
- **2,990** CNS Figure Captions & **307** complete CNS Methods / STAR★METHODS sections
- Curated *Nature* Summary Paragraph, MIT/Harvard CommKit *Discussion*, *Science* *Intro/Results*, and Elsevier *Cover Letter* editorial standards.

## Four Operating Modes (Pick Based on User Request)

### Mode A — Storyline Architect Mode (When Starting from Results / Outline / Revision)
**Always execute before drafting prose when the user shares experimental/omics Results or asks how to frame a paper:**
0. **Match Optimal Oncology Entry Point, Intro Part-2 Gap & Novelty Proof Triangle FIRST (`match-storyline`)** (see [references/12-oncology-storyline-and-gap-playbooks.md](./references/12-oncology-storyline-and-gap-playbooks.md) and [references/13-novelty-engineering-and-reviewer-defense.md](./references/13-novelty-engineering-and-reviewer-defense.md)):
   - Run `python scripts/audit_manuscript.py match-storyline --file results_notes.md` to pinpoint the **Top 2–3 sharpest scientific entry angles** across the **30-Entry-Point 6-Year Oncology Encyclopedia** (`n = 6,665`), check for a **Double-Axis CNS Elevation Combo** (Mainstream Anchor + `🔥 1.5–4.1×` growth frontier), **audit all 6 Proof-of-Novelty Results Modules (`P1–P6`) to preempt *"lacks novelty"* reviewer rejections**, lock the **`X, rather than Y`** conceptual delta + **Introduction Part-2 Gap**, and map the canonical `H1 → H7` Results chain + aligned Discussion elevation!
1. **Classify the Study Archetype** (see [references/00-strategic-narrative-design.md](./references/00-strategic-narrative-design.md)):
   - `Mechanistic Axis + Perturbation` | `Clinical Biomarker / Therapy` | `Atlas / Spatial Resource` | `Evolution / Metastasis` | `Computational / Tech Method`
2. **Identify the Target Journal Tier** (`general` [default `~6,000w` Main Text when no target journal is set!], `nature`, `nat_cell_biol`, `nat_commun`, `nat_cancer`, `nat_med`, `nat_methods`, `nature_sister`, `cell`, `cancer_cell`, `immunity`, `science`) because journal rules strictly govern Title length/colons, Abstract word caps (`125` vs `150` vs `200w`), **section raw + paren-stripped pure-prose word budgets**, **per-journal `P/q`, non-`P` metric, and parenthesis caps**, Subheading style (`5w` phrase vs `10w` full clause), Main Figure caps (`5` vs `7`), Supplementary vs Extended Data naming, and Limitations placement.
3. **Reverse-Engineer the Whole Manuscript from Results** (never draft Intro from textbook background!):
   - Extract the **2–3 core tension entities** from the Results → prune Introduction down to ONLY those entities (`P1` broad hook `10%` overlap → `P2–P3` focused tension `37%` overlap → strong non-trivial Gap → `Here, we...` preview).
   - Propose **3 ranked Title candidates** (Conceptual Causal vs Discovery-Target Hybrid vs Clinical/Tool Brand) matched to the journal.
   - Map the **6–7 Results Subheading Domino Chain** (`H1` Discovery/Cohort → `H2–H5` Spatial/Molecular Mechanism → `H6–H7` In Vivo Rescue / Clinical Translation).
   - Design the **Discussion 4-Layer Elevation** (Unified Conceptual Model → Paradox Reconciliation → Actionable Translational Roadmap → Constructive 3-Part Limitations).

### Mode B — Corpus-First Section Drafting & Idiomatic Polishing Mode
**Dual-Wing Rule (Positive Corpus Retrieval FIRST + Guardrail Verification SECOND — Full-Paper Coverage)**:
1. Load ONLY the relevant section reference file(s) from the table below **PLUS** [references/08-comprehensive-phrasebank-and-collocations.md](./references/08-comprehensive-phrasebank-and-collocations.md) (Part 5, Scenarios 19–25) and [references/16-full-results-corpus-and-anti-stiffness-id.md](./references/16-full-results-corpus-and-anti-stiffness-id.md) (14-Module Full-Paper Results Atlas + Anti-Stiffness Intro/Discussion Playbook, distilled from `1,441` published 2025–2026 CNS papers).
2. **Retrieve & adapt positive CNS corpus frames BEFORE free-generating across ALL sections**:
   - **Figure Captions (`~96%+` corpus-coverable)**: assemble directly from the **21-Chart Corpus + Part 4 Closing Blocks** in [references/06-figure-captions.md](./references/06-figure-captions.md) (plugging in only verified `n`, panel labels, and exact rendered axis metrics).
   - **Methods / STAR★METHODS (`~95%+` corpus-coverable)**: assemble directly from the **23 Past-Passive Experimental & Computational Modules + Part 5 Availability Blocks** in [references/07-methods-and-star.md](./references/07-methods-and-star.md) (plugging in only real verified reagents, versions, and parameters).
   - **Results (`~95%`) & Intro/Discussion**: retrieve verified high-frequency verbs (Scenario 20), prepositional 3/4-grams (Scenario 21), all 8 natural Results opener families + cross-section bridges (Scenario 22 & `16` M14), Title-locked low-acronym front matter (Scenario 19 & `16` §2.4), natural `11–17w` short sentences (Scenario 24), and non-enumerative Discussion pivots + epistemic hypotheses (Scenario 25).
3. Enforce each section's non-negotiable grammar/tense/verb-ladder rules, eliminate Chinglish patterns, and preserve scientific precision.

Run [`scripts/audit_manuscript.py`](./scripts/audit_manuscript.py) to catch word-limit overflows, weak gaps, subject-style figure citations (`Fig. 1 shows`), verb overclaiming, P1 raw-stat repetition in Discussion, missing Cell Press Limitations, Cover Letter hype/compliance omissions, or Figure Caption / Methods reproducibility flaws:

### Mode D — Data/Results-Doc → Millimeter-Bounded Storyboard → Full Manuscript Expansion (`sci-plot` Linkage!)
**Use whenever the user supplies raw data tables, analysis outputs, or a Results description document and asks to plan Main/Supplementary figures AND expand into a full manuscript** (see [references/11-results-to-manuscript-and-figure-packing.md](./references/11-results-to-manuscript-and-figure-packing.md)):
1. **Extract Atomic Findings (Zero Fabrication)**: Parse every finding, sample size `n`, quantitative stat, assay, and causal tier (Tier 1–4); insert `[TODO: confirm ...]` for any missing stat rather than fabricating numbers.
2. **Triage Main vs Extended Data/Supp + Visual Hero Reservation + Millimeter Packing Check (`plan-figures`)**:
   - Every journal caps physical full-page canvas size (*Nature* `180×220 mm`, max `5` Main Figs; *Cell/Cancer Cell* `174×215 mm`, max `7` Main Figs; *Science* `184×218 mm`, max `6` Main Figs) and enforces **strict `1-Panel = 1-Unique-Letter` indexing (`90.4%` CNS standard — never bundle distinct charts under one letter!)**.
   - **Real Visual Evidence ("Seeing is Believing", `65.1%` of CNS manuscripts)**: When the user has clinical radiology (`MRI/CT/PET`), histopathology (`H&E`, `IHC`), multiplex microscopy (`mIF/CODEX/IMC`), gross tumor/IVIS, or organoid images, protect representative visual anchors at **Tier-1 priority** and **reserve `35–55%` of the Main Figure's physical space** (`176×66–78 mm` full-width Hero Band or `115×58–68 mm` 2/3-width Hero Block) synced 1:1 with `sci-plot`. When it is unclear whether real imaging exists, proactively recommend `1–3` highest-impact visual anchors matched to the study type!
   - Run `python scripts/audit_manuscript.py plan-figures --journal <target> --input draft_storyboard.json --rebalance --out final_storyboard.json` to verify 1:1 panel letters, real-image hero space, and page-height overflow, executing the **4-Tier Rebalancing Protocol**: (1) Demote QC/gating/toxicity/null/extra-imaging replicates/full-dotplot panels to paired `ED Fig. / Fig. S`, (2) Recommend `sci-plot` high-density Track-B merges (e.g., ≥3 volcanoes → 1 `plot_multi_cluster_volcano`), (3) Split overflowing high-priority modules into a spare Main Fig slot, or (4) Compress Main Figs when converting 7-fig drafts to 5-fig *Nature*.
3. **Lock Shared `final_storyboard.json` → Expand Full Manuscript Synchronously**:
   - Once `0 OVERFLOW` is confirmed, expand `Results` → `Figure Captions` → `Introduction` → `Discussion` → `Title/Abstract/Highlights/In brief` → `Methods/STAR` → `Cover Letter` with 1:1 synchronized panel IDs (`Fig. 1a`, `Extended Data Fig. 2b`), and pass the same `final_storyboard.json` `(w_mm, h_mm)` dimensions straight to `sci-plot` for 1:1 millimeter plotting!

## Twelve Non-Negotiable Full-Manuscript CNS Laws

1. **Journal-Calibrated Titles** ([01-title-and-abstract.md](./references/01-title-and-abstract.md)): *Nature* `~9w / 0% colons`, *Science* `~10w / 0% colons`, *Cell/Cancer Cell* `11–12w`, *Nat Med* `~14w / 46% clinical colon / 2.4% declarative` (no hype!), *Nat Methods* `ToolName: ...` (`38%` colon). Mechanistic papers use active causal clauses (`84.3%`: `drives`, `promotes`, `controls`, `reprograms`). Never open with `"Study on..."` or `"Analysis of..."`.
2. **Findings-Centered, Anti-Methods-Bloat Abstracts** ([01-title-and-abstract.md](./references/01-title-and-abstract.md)): Hard caps: *Nature* `~200w`, *Nat Med* `~218w`, *Nature* sisters + *Cell*/*Cancer Cell* `≤150w`, *Science* `≤125w`. Allocate ~50% of sentences to core quantitative findings (`Here, we show/identify/present...`), compress Methods to ≤1 sentence (`3.5%` empirical share), and never write a mini-full-text methods log.
3. **Reverse-Engineered Introduction Funnel & Zero Weak Gaps** ([02-introduction.md](./references/02-introduction.md)): `3–4` paragraphs (`Nature ~390w`, `Cell/Cancer Cell ~470–530w`). Introduce ONLY the 2–3 concepts paid off in Results. Never use weak `"few studies have investigated"` gaps (`1.1%` in 2025–2026 CNS); anchor Gaps in mechanistic unknown (`67%`), spatial/single-cell blindspot (`60%`), clinical resistance (`53%`), or technical bottleneck (`22%`). Respect 3-tense split (Present `55%` / Past `31%` / Present Perfect `15%`).
4. **Journal-Matched Results Subheadings (`6–7` Domino Modules)** ([03-results-and-subheadings.md](./references/03-results-and-subheadings.md)): *Nature* uses crisp `5–7` word headlines; *Cell / Cancer Cell / Immunity / Sci Transl Med* use `10–12` word active declarative takeaway sentences (`52–68%`). Order logically so each module's discovery motivates the next (`To investigate/determine/test...`).
5. **Inline Parenthetical Figure Citations (`765 : 1`) & Strict Results Past Tense (`4.9 : 1`)** ([03-results-and-subheadings.md](./references/03-results-and-subheadings.md)): Always cite figures parenthetically at clause end (`...increased tumor infiltration (Fig. 1a)`), **NEVER** as sentence subjects (`"Fig. 1a shows..."`). Write Results observations in Simple Past; avoid `data not shown` (`2.9%`).
6. **4-Tier Certainty Verb Ladder — Never Overclaim or Randomly Swap Verbs** ([03-results-and-subheadings.md](./references/03-results-and-subheadings.md)): Match verb strength strictly to evidence: Genetic rescue/in vivo causality (`demonstrated`, `established`, `abolished`, `rescued`, `drove`) > Direct assay/omics observation (`showed`, `identified`, `enriched`, `increased`, `induced`) > Computational inference (`indicated`, `suggested`, `supported`, `consistent with`) > Correlative cohort observation (`associated with`, `correlated with`, `linked to`, `may/might`). Avoid `"proved"` (`<0.1%`).
7. **Zero-Repetition, 4-Layer Elevated Discussion** ([04-discussion.md](./references/04-discussion.md)): Follow MIT/Harvard 5 blocks. **99.6%** of top-journal Discussion P1s do NOT repeat raw P values or percentages — open with a 2–3 sentence unified conceptual model answering Introduction's big question. Reconcile prior literature (`Consistent with...`, `Whereas prior studies..., our data...`), specify concrete translational utility (`77%`), and obey journal Limitations norms (**mandatory `Limitations of the study` subheading in Cell Press** `85–98%`, standalone paragraph in *Nat Med* `70%`, woven constructively in *Nature* `26%`).
8. **30-Second Editor-Focused Cover Letter** ([05-cover-letter.md](./references/05-cover-letter.md)): Strictly 1 page (`300–500` words). Targeted Editor greeting; 2–3 sentence strategic breakthrough (**never** paste the Abstract); specific journal-scope fit citing 1–2 recent papers from that journal; 4 mandatory compliance statements (originality, COI, funding, ethics); zero empty superlatives (`groundbreaking`, `first ever`).
9. **Telegraphic Participial Figure Captions (`5 : 1` `-ing`)** ([06-figure-captions.md](./references/06-figure-captions.md)): Exact journal syntax (*Nature* `Fig. 1 | ...` + `a, b,` + British spelling + `≤350w`; *Cell* `Figure 1. ...` + `(A)` + American spelling); mandatory `n` + biological unit, boxplot/error-bar anatomy, two-sided test + FDR, scale bars, and Source Data.
10. **Past-Passive Reproducible Methods (`9.89 : 1`)** ([07-methods-and-star.md](./references/07-methods-and-star.md)): Match *Nature* Online Methods vs *Cell* STAR★METHODS (+ Key Resources Table) vs *Science*; disclose exact software versions, genome builds, IRB/IACUC, STR/mycoplasma authentication, patient-level/pseudobulk stats, and permanent data/code accessions.
11. **Empirical Main-Text Reference Waterfall & Anti-Under-Citation Gate (`Med = 62` Main Refs)** ([09-citation-and-reference-architecture.md](./references/09-citation-and-reference-architecture.md)): Never leave a high-IF manuscript starved at ~35–40 main-text citations (`75.8%` of 2024–2026 CNS papers cite `≥50` unique refs in Main Text alone: *Cell* `69`, *Immunity* `68`, *Cancer Cell* `66`, *Nat Cancer* `54`, *Nature* `53` + `~27` Methods). Build naturally via the empirical waterfall: **Intro `20–24` unique** (`62–69%` of P1–P3 sentences cited) + **Results `+21–32` NEW unique** (`3–5` per subheading across the 7 inline triggers: signatures/markers, algorithms, cohorts, mouse/assay models, drugs, premise pivots, prior concordance) + **Discussion `+11–15` NEW unique** (trials, cross-cancer extensions, reconciliation + `~17%` looped Intro refs) + **Methods `+12–25` NEW**; keep `95.1%` of anchors to `1–3` refs, `~47%` within 5 years, **`0` orphan uncited references**, and NEVER fabricate fake references (use verifiable `[CITE: ...]` slots until matched to real PubMed/DOI entries).
12. **Dual-Wing Corpus-First Realism, Front-Matter Acronym Discipline & Per-Journal Pure-Prose Calibration (`n = 1,441` + `550` Papers)** ([08-comprehensive-phrasebank-and-collocations.md](./references/08-comprehensive-phrasebank-and-collocations.md) Part 4–5, [14-anti-ai-prose-stat-restraint-and-rule-harmony.md](./references/14-anti-ai-prose-stat-restraint-and-rule-harmony.md) & [15-journal-format-and-standards-matrix.md](./references/15-journal-format-and-standards-matrix.md)): **Combine BOTH wings — retrieve verified positive CNS verbs, prepositional 3/4-grams, and paragraph transitions FIRST (`08` Scenarios 19–25) instead of free-generating, AND pass every per-journal guardrail!**
    - **Front-Matter Acronym, Code & Title-Lock Restraint (`08` Scenario 19, `16` §2.4)**: Keep **Title `0–1` acronym** (`<3`), **Abstract `≤ 5–7` acronyms/100w** (`≤ 2–3` parens, **ZERO internal codes** like `MP4/CN8/Model A`, **AND `≥ 60–75%` Title signature-term recall in Abstract** so Title & Abstract stay tightly locked!), and **Introduction `≤ 6–7` acronyms/100w** (`0` Results-only codes).
    - **Per-Journal Length & Parenthesis Budgets**: Calibrate ALL length, Abstract cap, `P/q`, non-`P` stat, and parenthesis thresholds to the target journal — or default to **`general` (`~6,000w` raw / `≥ 4,600w` paren-stripped pure prose)** when no target journal is set; keep Results paren share `≤ 18%` (`med 12.4%`) and put 100% of exact stats on Figures/Captions/Source Data.
    - **Human Progression, Cross-Section Bridges & Discussion Depth (`08` Scenarios 22–25, `16` §2.4)**: Rotate all **8 natural Results paragraph-opener families** (`We next/then` `5–15%`, `To [verb]` `12–25%`, **gerund/participle openers `≤ 15%`** [no anti-`We next` overcorrection!], **active `We` in `45–70%` of paragraph opening sentences**, **`0` `≥ 5` bare-NP streaks**, **1-clause biological motivation bridge at every `H_k → H_{k+1}` cross-section boundary**, offload raw `GSE/NCT` accessions to Figures/Methods), use natural `11–17w` sentences (`CV 0.44–0.55`, mean `25–29w`, `0` em-dashes, `;` inside `(n = X; Fig. Ya)`), cap Discussion `First/Second` enumeration to **`≤ 1` paragraph** with genuine epistemic mechanistic speculation (`6–10/1kw`), verify **`0` uncited Results subheadings**, **zero phantom scale bars**, and **100% plot-axis/schematic ↔ text/legend cross-check** (Conflict Matrix: 17 pairs in `14`).
