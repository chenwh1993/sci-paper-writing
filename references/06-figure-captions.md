### 2. Exact Typography & Symbol Micro-Rules

1. **En-dash (`–`, U+2013) vs Hyphen (`-`)**:
   - Use **en-dash** for ranges (`**a–c**,`, `n = 10–15`, `days 0–21`) and compound names (`Wilcoxon rank-sum`, `Mann–Whitney`, `Kaplan–Meier`, `Kruskal–Wallis`, `Benjamini–Hochberg`, `log-rank` uses hyphen, `cell–cell`, `ligand–receptor`, `dose–response`).
   - Use **true minus sign (`−`, U+2212)** for negative values (`−log₁₀(P)`, `−2 to +2`, `−80 °C`).
2. **Spacing around Operators & Units**:
   - Single space around `=`, `<`, `>`, `≤`, `≥`, `±`, `×` (except `1.5× IQR` or `2×` multiplier where no space precedes `×`).
   - Single space between number and physical unit (`50 μm`, `24 h`, `10 mg kg⁻¹` or `10 mg/kg`), **no space** before `%` (`95% CI`) or `°` in `°C` (`37 °C` has space before `°C`).
3. **Gene vs Protein Italics** (strict across all CNS journals):
   - Human gene symbols: **ALL-CAPS italic** (*TP53*, *PDCD1*, *CXCL13*); human proteins: **ALL-CAPS upright** (TP53, PD-1, CXCL13).
   - Mouse gene symbols: **Title-case italic** (*Trp53*, *Pdcd1*, *Cxcl13*); mouse proteins: **ALL-CAPS upright** (TRP53, PD-1, CXCL13).
   - Species names & Latin abbreviations: *Homo sapiens*, *Mus musculus*, *in vivo*, *in vitro*, *ex vivo*, *de novo*, *versus* (or `vs.`).
4. **BioRender / Adapted Artwork Credit**:
   - When a schematic panel was created with BioRender, append `Created with BioRender.com.` (or `Schematic in a created with BioRender.com.`) — empirically observed across *Nature*, *Cell*, and *Science*.
   - When adapted from a published figure: `Adapted with permission from ref. X.`
5. **Scale-Bar Truthfulness & Working-Model Numerical Cross-Check (Mandatory Pre-Submission Gate!)**:
   - **Zero Phantom Scale Bars**: NEVER write `(scale bar, 2 cm)` or `(scale bar, 5 mm)` in a Figure Legend unless a calibrated physical scale bar is **genuinely drawn and visibly verified** on the rendered image! For clinical/preclinical MRI, CT, or microscopy panels lacking an embedded bar: either draw a verified vector scale bar from exact DICOM/OME-TIFF pixel spacing, or truthfully state the exact physical field of view (`FOV, 18 × 18 cm`) instead of claiming a non-existent scale bar.
   - **100% Schematic ↔ Data-Panel Consistency**: Cross-check EVERY numerical distance (`µm`), score (`AUC`, `C-index`, `HR`), `P` value, sample size `n`, and gene/cell-type label printed inside schematics (`Fig. 1a`, experimental timelines, `Working Model`) against the corresponding data panels so zero numerical drift occurs.

### 3. Structural Blueprint of a 300-Word CNS Figure Caption

```text
[1. BOLD TITLE: 8–14 words, Sentence case, declarative takeaway or concise NP]
[2. PANEL WALKTHROUGH: 1–2 crisp telegraphic sentences per panel or panel group;
    explain what is plotted + visual encodings (color, size, shape, dashed lines)
    + only panel-specific n/conditions]
[3. SHARED DISCLOSURES (1 compact paragraph): boxplot anatomy / error-bar definition
    + replicate definition + statistical test(s) with sidedness & FDR adjustment
    + significance key (*P < 0.05 ... NS) + scale bars if microscopy]
[4. CROSS-REFERENCES & ABBREVIATIONS: See also Extended Data Fig. X / Figure S1;
    Source data statement; non-standard abbreviation key if needed]
```

## Part 2 — CNS Figure Caption Grammar, Tense Rules & Vocabulary Bank

Empirically mined from **2,990 CNS & high-impact figure captions** (`n = 2,091` Main + `892` Extended Data). Use this guide to write native, concise, grammatically exact CNS English.

---

### 1. Tense & Voice Decision Matrix

| Caption Component | Grammatical Form | Why / Empirical Rule | Authentic CNS Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bold Title** (Declarative) | **Simple Present (Active)** | States an established biological finding supported by the figure | `IL-27R agonism drives a cytotoxic CD8+ T cell state in tumours.` |
| **Bold Title** (Descriptive) | **Compact Noun Phrase** (no finite verb) | Favored in *Nature* (`72%` of main figures) for atlas/landscape figures | `Single-cell transcriptional and spatial landscape of human colorectal cancer.` |
| **Panel Lead-in** | **Telegraphic Noun Phrase + Present Participle (`-ing`)** | `showing` (`2,203`) beats `shows` (`452`) by **5 : 1**; omits "Panel a shows..." | `**b**, Heatmap showing row-scaled expression of top marker genes across clusters.` |
| **Visual Encoding / Legend Key** | **Simple Present** (`represents`, `indicates`, `denotes`, `corresponds to`, `reflects`) | Describes permanent visual properties on the page | `Each dot represents an individual patient; dot size indicates the percentage of expressing cells.` |
| **Experimental Procedure / Cohort Provenance** | **Simple Past Passive** (`were collected`, `were treated`, `were sorted`, `were stained`) | Refers to completed historical wet-lab actions | `**c**, Tumour growth curves of mice treated with anti-PD-1 or isotype control.` / `Cells were sorted from tumour-draining lymph nodes.` |
| **Statistical Computation** | **Simple Past Passive** (`were calculated`, `were determined`, `were compared`, `were adjusted`) OR **Telegraphic Nominal** | Both are standard; nominal saves words in *Nature* | `Two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test.` OR `P values were calculated using a two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test.` |

### 3. Empirically Ranked Panel Openers (Top 35 Authentic CNS Constructions)

Never start a panel with `"Panel a shows..."` or `"This figure shows..."`. Open directly with one of these proven nominal/participial stems:

1. **Quantification & Comparison**:
   - `Quantification of [metric] in [groups/conditions]...` (`#1` most frequent opener)
   - `Comparison of [feature] between [group A] and [group B]...`
   - `Proportion / Percentage / Frequency / Abundance of [cell type] among [parent population]...`
   - `Distribution of [score/metric] across [clusters/cohorts]...`
   - `Relative expression / abundance / levels of [gene/protein] in [groups]...`
   - `Number of [events/genes/clones] detected in [conditions]...`
   - `Fold change in [metric] relative to [control]...`
2. **Overview & Schematics**:
   - `Schematic of [the experimental design / study workflow / mechanism]...`
   - `Overview of [the patient cohort / multi-omics dataset / analytical pipeline]...`
   - `Experimental design / setup for [assay/model]...`
3. **Chart-Named Openers**:
   - `UMAP projection / embedding / plot of [N cells] colored by [annotation]...`
   - `Heatmap showing [row-scaled expression / correlation / enrichment] of [features]...`
   - `Dot plot showing [mean expression and fraction of expressing cells] for [markers]...`
   - `Box plots / Violin plots comparing [score] across [conditions]...`
   - `Bar plot / Stacked bar plot illustrating [proportions / enrichment scores]...`
   - `Volcano plot highlighting differentially expressed genes between [A] and [B]...`
   - `Scatter plot showing the correlation between [X] and [Y]...`
   - `Kaplan–Meier survival curves of patients stratified by [signature/group]...`
   - `Forest plot showing multivariable Cox hazard ratios for [outcome]...`
   - `Receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves evaluating [model] in [cohort]...`
4. **Imaging & Flow Cytometry**:
   - `Representative immunofluorescence / IHC / H&E images of [tissue] stained for [markers]...`
   - `Representative flow cytometry plots (left) and quantification (right) of [population]...`
   - `Confocal micrographs showing [localization] in [condition]...`
5. **Cross-Panel Condensation (Space Saver!)**:
   - `Same as in **a**, but for [validation cohort / independent dataset / CD4+ T cells].`
   - `As in **(A)**, showing [stratification by treatment arm].`

### 5. Common Chinglish / Non-Idiomatic Pitfalls → Authentic CNS Fixes

| # | ❌ Avoid (Chinglish / Wordy / Ambiguous) | ✅ Authentic CNS Replacement | Why |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `This figure shows the UMAP result of T cells.` | `UMAP projection of T cells (n = 42,150) colored by subcluster.` | Drops redundant `"This figure shows"`; adds cell count & encoding. |
| 2 | `Panel A shows a heatmap of marker genes.` | `**a**, Heatmap showing row-scaled expression of marker genes.` | Telegraphic NP + `-ing` (`5:1` CNS norm). |
| 3 | `Red means high expression and blue means low expression.` | `Color scale indicates row-scaled z-scores of normalized expression.` | Objective quantitative scale rather than informal color narration. |
| 4 | `The expression of CXCL13 was significantly higher in responders, suggesting its key role.` | `**c**, Comparison of *CXCL13* expression between responders (n = 23) and non-responders (n = 21).` | Captions describe *what is shown*, not Discussion-level speculation (`suggesting...`). |
| 5 | `T-test was used to calculate the p value.` | `Two-sided unpaired Student's t-test.` | Specifies sidedness + paired/unpaired; avoids passive bloat. |
| 6 | `P value was shown on the top of the figure.` | `Exact *P* values are indicated above the brackets.` | Idiomatic preposition + exact academic noun (`brackets`). |
| 7 | `P = 0.000` or `P < 0.000` | `*P* < 0.0001` or `*P* = 2.3 × 10⁻⁵` | A *P* value can never equal zero. |
| 8 | `Error bars show the variance of data.` | `Data are presented as mean ± s.d. (n = 5 biological replicates).` | "Variance" is ambiguous; specify exact estimator (`s.d.` vs `s.e.m.`) + `n`. |
| 9 | `Each dot is one sample.` | `Each dot represents an individual patient.` | `represents` (`937` hits) + exact biological unit (`patient`, not vague `sample`). |
| 10 | `Box plot shows the distribution of score.` *(no box anatomy)* | `Box plots show the median (centre line), IQR (box limits), and whiskers extending to 1.5× IQR.` | Mandatory Nature/Cell/Science boxplot disclosure. |
| 11 | `The size of circle means percentage.` | `Dot size indicates the percentage of cells expressing the indicated gene.` | Standard CNS dotplot phrasing. |
| 12 | `Black dash line is the threshold.` | `Dashed vertical lines denote \|log₂(fold change)\| = 1 and FDR = 0.05.` | Correct participle `dashed` + explicit numeric cutoff. |
| 13 | `Scale bar = 50um.` | `Scale bar, 50 μm.` | Comma style, true Greek mu (`μ`), space before unit, no `=`. |
| 14 | `The same analysis was also done in GSE12345.` | `Same as in **a**, but for the external validation cohort (GSE12345; n = 84).` | Canonical CNS cross-panel condensation (`Same as in`). |
| 15 | `NS means no significance.` | `NS, not significant.` | Exact standardized CNS legend key. |
| 16 | `Figure 1. Single-Cell Analysis Of Tumor Microenvironment In Cancer.` | `**Figure 1. Single-cell atlas of the human colorectal tumor microenvironment**` | Modern CNS uses Sentence case (`96.5–99.2%`), not Title Case. |
| 17 | `n = 15,000 cells` *(when testing patient responders vs non-responders)* | `n = 18 responders and n = 16 non-responders (pseudobulk patient-level analysis)` | Prevents reviewer rejection for single-cell pseudoreplication. |
| 18 | `Kaplan-Meier plot showed better survival in high group.` | `Kaplan–Meier curves comparing overall survival between *CXCL13*-high (n = 48) and *CXCL13*-low (n = 52) patients (two-sided log-rank test).` | En-dash in `Kaplan–Meier`, states endpoint (`overall survival`), group `n`, and test. |
| 19 | `Representative image of IHC staining.` *(no replicate count or scale bar)* | `Representative IHC micrographs of [marker] in tumour sections from n = 6 patients per group. Scale bar, 100 μm.` | Satisfies microscopy reproducibility disclosure. |
| 20 | `Correlation analysis between A and B (r = 0.65).` | `Scatter plot showing the Spearman correlation between [A] and [B] (n = 64 patients; two-sided test; shaded band, 95% CI).` | Names correlation type (`Spearman` vs `Pearson`), `n`, sidedness, and shading. |

## Part 3 — Reusable CNS Figure Caption Corpus by Chart Type

Empirically distilled reusable sentence stems from **2,990 CNS & high-impact captions**. Replace bracketed `[SLOTS]` with study-specific nouns; adjust panel tag style (`**a**,` for Nature; `**(A)**` for Cell/Science) and spelling (`tumour`/`colour`/`s.d.` vs `tumor`/`color`/`SD`).

---

### 1. UMAP / t-SNE & Single-Cell Atlas (`18.5%` of CNS figures)
- **Standard Cluster UMAP**:
  - `UMAP projection of [N] [cells/nuclei] from [M] [patients/samples], colored by [major lineage / cell subcluster].`
  - `Uniform manifold approximation and projection (UMAP) embedding of [N] [T cells] showing [K] transcriptionally distinct subclusters.`
- **Multi-Subcluster Composite with Major Inset**:
  - `UMAP visualization of [N] [single cells] colored by [K] fine-grained subclusters. Inset (top left) shows the same embedding colored by major cell lineages.`
- **Feature / Gene Expression Overlay**:
  - `UMAP plots showing normalized expression of canonical marker genes (*[GENE1]*, *[GENE2]*, *[GENE3]*); color intensity reflects log₂-transformed expression.`
  - `Same as in **a**, colored by [tissue origin / treatment response / sample time point].`
- **Density / Differential Abundance UMAP**:
  - `UMAP embedding colored by local cell-density enrichment (`log₂` fold change) between [Group A] and [Group B] (Milo neighborhood analysis; FDR < 0.1).`

---

### 2. Heatmap & Clustered Matrix (`18.9%` of CNS figures)
- **Marker / Gene Expression Heatmap**:
  - `Heatmap showing row-scaled z-scores of [top N differentially expressed genes / canonical lineage markers] across [K] [cell subclusters / patient groups].`
  - `Unsupervised hierarchical clustering of [N] [samples] based on [feature set]. Top annotation bars indicate [clinical stage, treatment response, and molecular subtype].`
- **Correlation / Similarity / Enrichment Matrix**:
  - `Heatmap of pairwise Spearman correlation coefficients (`ρ`) among [K] [cell subsets / gene signatures]; asterisks indicate BH-adjusted *P* < 0.05.`
  - `Heatmap depicting tissue preference (`R_o/e`, ratio of observed to expected cell numbers) of each [T cell subcluster] across [tissue types].`

---

### 3. Dot Plot / Bubble Plot (`7.0%` of CNS figures)
- **Single-Cell Marker Dot Plot**:
  - `Dot plot showing the expression of selected [marker/functional] genes across [K] [cell clusters]. Dot size indicates the percentage of cells expressing each gene, and color intensity represents the scaled average expression (z-score).`
- **Pathway / GSEA / Enrichment Dot Plot**:
  - `Bubble plot of significantly enriched [Hallmark / GO / KEGG] pathways in [Group A] versus [Group B]. Dot size corresponds to [gene ratio / gene count], and color denotes [normalized enrichment score (NES) / −log₁₀(FDR)].`

---

### 4. Box Plot & Violin Plot (`20.5%` of CNS figures)
- **Group Comparison Box Plot**:
  - `Box plots comparing [cell frequency (% of CD45⁺ cells) / signature score / gene expression] across [Group A (n = X), Group B (n = Y), and Group C (n = Z)]. Each dot represents an individual [patient/sample].`
- **Paired Before/After Comparison**:
  - `Paired comparison of [metric] in pre-treatment and on-treatment [tumour biopsies] (n = [X] matched patients). Grey lines connect paired samples from the same patient; two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test.`
- **Violin Plot (Single-Cell / Distribution)**:
  - `Violin plots displaying the distribution of [cytotoxicity / exhaustion module score] across [cell subclusters]; internal box plots indicate the median and interquartile range (IQR).`
- **Mandatory Boxplot Anatomy Line** *(see `stats-and-disclosure-corpus.md` for variants)*:
  - `Box plots show the median (centre line), interquartile range (IQR; box limits, 25th and 75th percentiles), and whiskers extending to 1.5× IQR.`

---

### 5. Bar Plot & Stacked Composition Plot (`11.6%` of CNS figures)
- **Grouped / Single Bar with Overlaid Points**:
  - `Bar plot showing [metric] in [cells/mice] treated with [conditions] (n = [X] biological replicates per group). Bars represent mean ± s.d. with individual data points overlaid.`
- **Stacked Composition Bar Plot**:
  - `Stacked bar plot illustrating the relative proportion of [major cell lineages / subclusters] across [patients / tissue sites / treatment arms].`
  - `Bar graph comparing the fraction of [responders] versus [non-responders] stratified by [biomarker status] (two-sided Fisher's exact test).`
- **Horizontal Pathway / Enrichment Bar Plot**:
  - `Horizontal bar plot of top enriched pathways ranked by [normalized enrichment score (NES) / −log₁₀(adjusted *P*)]; dashed vertical line denotes FDR = 0.05.`

---

### 6. Volcano Plot & MA Plot (`3.4%` of CNS figures)
- **Differential Expression Volcano**:
  - `Volcano plot showing differentially expressed genes (DEGs) between [Group A] and [Group B] (pseudobulk DESeq2, n = [X] vs [Y] patients). Dashed lines indicate thresholds of |log₂(fold change)| = [1] and Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted *P* = [0.05]; top upregulated (red) and downregulated (blue) genes are labelled.`
- **Proteomics / Perturbation Screen Volcano**:
  - `Volcano plot depicting log₂ fold enrichment versus −log₁₀(FDR) of [candidate interactors / sgRNA targets] comparing [perturbed] against [control] conditions (n = [X] independent replicates).`

---

### 7. Scatter Plot & Correlation (`8.1%` of CNS figures)
- **Continuous Association**:
  - `Scatter plot showing the [Spearman / Pearson] correlation between [Variable X] and [Variable Y] across [n = X patients / samples]. Solid line indicates linear regression fit; shaded band denotes the 95% confidence interval; two-sided correlation test.`
- **Fold-Change / Cross-Cohort Comparison**:
  - `Scatter plot comparing log₂(fold change) of [genes/features] in [Cohort 1] versus [Cohort 2]; dotted diagonal line marks the identity line (*y* = *x*).`

---

### 8. Survival Curves (Kaplan–Meier) & Cox Forest Plot (`5.7%` of CNS figures)
- **Kaplan–Meier Survival Curve**:
  - `Kaplan–Meier curves comparing [overall survival (OS) / progression-free survival (PFS)] between patients with high (n = [X]) versus low (n = [Y]) [signature score / cell abundance] in the [cohort name] cohort (stratified by [median / optimal cutoff]; two-sided log-rank test). Shaded bands indicate 95% confidence intervals; numbers at risk are listed below.`
- **Multivariable Cox Forest Plot**:
  - `Forest plot showing multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression for [overall survival] adjusted for [age, sex, and clinical stage] (n = [X] patients). Squares represent hazard ratios (HRs), horizontal error bars denote 95% confidence intervals, and the dashed vertical line marks HR = 1.`

## Part 4 — Mandatory Statistical, Dispersion & Reproducibility Disclosure Corpus

Ready-to-paste, reviewer-proof closing blocks distilled verbatim-in-structure from *Nature*, *Cell*, and *Science* editorial policies and 2,990 audited captions.

---

### 1. Box Plot Anatomy Definitions (Mandatory Whenever Any Box Plot Appears)

- **Nature Compact Style (Preferred when word-limited)**:
  - `Box plots show the median (centre line), interquartile range (IQR; box limits), and whiskers extending to 1.5× IQR.`
  - `For **c–e**, box plot elements are defined as: centre line, median; box limits, upper and lower quartiles (25th and 75th percentiles); whiskers, 1.5× interquartile range; points, individual patients.`
- **Cell / Science Full Style**:
  - `In all box plots, the center line represents the median, box limits denote the 25th and 75th percentiles (interquartile range, IQR), whiskers extend to the most extreme data points within 1.5× IQR, and individual dots represent [biological replicates / patients / outliers].`
- **Min-to-Max Whiskers (only if whiskers actually span full range)**:
  - `Center line, median; box limits, 25th and 75th percentiles; whiskers, minimum to maximum values.`

---

### 2. Center, Error Bars & Confidence Band Definitions

| Estimator | Nature Style | Cell / Science Style |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mean ± SD** (recommended for showing biological spread) | `Data are presented as mean ± s.d.` | `Data are presented as mean ± SD.` |
| **Mean ± SEM** (uncertainty of mean) | `Data are presented as mean ± s.e.m.` | `Data are presented as mean ± SEM.` |
| **Median with IQR** (skewed / non-normal data) | `Data are shown as median with interquartile range (IQR).` | `Horizontal lines indicate median and interquartile range (IQR).` |
| **95% Confidence Interval** (curves / regressions / HR) | `Shaded bands / Error bars represent 95% confidence intervals (CIs).` | `Shaded regions / Error bars denote 95% confidence intervals (95% CI).` |
| **Combined multi-panel phrasing** | `In **b–d**, bars and error bars represent mean ± s.d. of n = [X] biological replicates.` | `In (B)–(D), data represent mean ± SEM from n = [X] mice per group.` |

---

### 3. Sample Size (`n`) & Replication Statements

Always define **both the count `n` and the exact biological unit**:
- **Clinical / Patient Cohorts**:
  - `n = [48] patients ([23] responders and [25] non-responders).`
  - `Each dot represents an individual patient (n = [X] per group); pseudobulk profiles were aggregated per patient prior to statistical testing.`
- **Animal Models**:
  - `n = [8] mice per group; data are pooled from [two] independent experiments.`
  - `n = [6] biological replicates (individual mice) per condition, representative of [three] independent experiments.`
- **In Vitro / Cell Lines / Organoids**:
  - `Data are from n = [3] biological replicates (independent cell cultures), representative of [three] independent experiments with similar results.`
  - `n = [4] patient-derived organoid lines tested in technical triplicate.`
- **Microscopy / Imaging**:
  - `Representative images from n = [5] patients per group ([three] fields of view per section). Scale bar, [50 μm].`
  - `Micrographs are representative of at least three independent experiments.`

---

### 4. Statistical Test Phrasing & Sidedness (Always State Two-Sided / Two-Tailed!)

#### 4.1 Single-Test Closing Sentences
- **Two-group non-parametric (unpaired)**:
  - `Two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test (Mann–Whitney *U*-test).`
- **Two-group non-parametric (paired)**:
  - `Two-sided Wilcoxon matched-pairs signed-rank test.`
- **Two-group parametric (unpaired / Welch / paired)**:
  - `Two-sided unpaired Student's *t*-test.`
  - `Two-sided Welch's *t*-test (unequal variances).`
  - `Two-sided paired Student's *t*-test.`
- **≥ 3 groups (non-parametric)**:
  - `Kruskal–Wallis test followed by Dunn's post hoc test with Benjamini–Hochberg correction.`
- **≥ 3 groups (parametric — one-way / two-way)**:
  - `One-way ANOVA followed by Tukey's multiple comparisons test.`
  - `Two-way ANOVA with Šídák's multiple comparisons test.`
  - `Two-way repeated-measures ANOVA (tumour growth curves).`
- **Correlation**:
  - `Two-sided Spearman's rank correlation test.` / `Two-sided Pearson's correlation test.`
- **Categorical / Proportions**:
  - `Two-sided Fisher's exact test.` / `Two-sided Pearson's χ² test.`
- **Survival**:
  - `Two-sided log-rank (Mantel–Cox) test.`
- **Multiple-Testing Adjustment (Omics / High-Dimensional)**:
  - `P values were adjusted for multiple testing using the Benjamini–Hochberg false discovery rate (FDR) method.`

#### 4.2 Compact Multi-Panel Statistical Routing (Saves 40–60 Words!)
- **Nature Style**:
  - `Statistical significance was determined by two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test (**b**, **c**), two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (**d**), Kruskal–Wallis test with Dunn's post hoc test (**e**), or two-sided log-rank test (**f**).`
- **Cell / Science Style**:
  - `Statistical significance was assessed using two-tailed unpaired Student's *t*-test (B), one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post hoc test (C and D), or two-sided log-rank test (F).`

---

### 5. *P*-Value & Significance Threshold Keys

- **When exact *P* values are printed on the figure (Preferred by *Nature* / *Science*)**:
  - `Exact *P* values are indicated on the graphs.`
  - `Exact *P* values are shown above the brackets; NS, not significant (*P* ≥ 0.05).`
- **When significance asterisks are used**:
  - `*P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001, ****P < 0.0001; NS, not significant.`
  - `Adjusted *P* values (FDR): *q < 0.05, **q < 0.01, ***q < 0.001; ns, not significant.`
- **When *P* values are moved to Source Data (dense multi-group grids)**:
  - `Exact *P* values and *n* numbers for all comparisons are provided in the Source Data.`
