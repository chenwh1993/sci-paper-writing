# Results & Subheadings Writing Rules, Verb Certainty Ladder & Cohesion Corpus

Empirically distilled from **1,442** 2025–2026 high-impact Results sections (**1,155** ordered subheading chains; **78,831** inline figure citations) benchmarked against *Science* / CNS Results standards.

## 1. Results Subheadings (`小标题`) — Journal-Matched Rules & Domino Arc

### 1.1 Journal-Specific Subheading Specifications (`n = 1,155`)
Across virtually every flagship journal, Results organize into **6–7 subheaded modules** (matching 6–7 main figures), but **syntax diverges sharply** between *Nature* main and *Cell / Immunity / Science* sisters:

| Target Journal | Median Modules | Median Words | Declarative Clause % | Exact Style Rule |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Nature** | **7** | **5w** (`4–7w`) | 30.8% | **Telegraphic headline** — crisp NP or ultra-short active clause (`Diminished p53 and FBP1 in human HCC`; `FBP1 loss licenses tumorigenesis through AKT`) |
| **Nat Genet / Nat Cancer / Nat Med / Nat Methods** | **6–7** | **7–8w** | 28.5–43.2% | Compact declarative claim or structured task/trial label (`Clinical trial design and baseline patient characteristics`; `Loki Align aligns ST-to-ST and H&E data`) |
| **Cell / Cancer Cell / Cell Rep Med / Sci Adv** | **6–7** | **10w** (`8–13w`) | **51.1–52.5%** | **Complete declarative biological takeaway sentence** (`EZH2 inhibition improves the anti-tumor efficacy of CAR-T cells in human B cell lymphoma models`) |
| **Immunity / Sci Immunol / Sci Transl Med / J Exp Med / Mol Cell** | **6–7** | **11–12w** | **56.6–82.2%** | **Strong active causal sentence** stating the exact immunological/mechanistic finding |

### 1.2 The 6–7 Module Narrative Domino Arc
Each subheading's output should logically motivate the next subheading's question:
- **H1 (Cohort / Atlas / Phenotype Discovery)**: Establish cohort scale, unbiased screen, or primary TME/clinical phenotype (`Single-cell and spatial profiling defines...` / `A unique macrophage population is enriched in...`).
- **H2–H3 (Cellular State, Spatial Architecture & Ligand–Receptor Niche)**: Zoom into the key subpopulation, spatial colocalization, or multicellular neighborhood (`SPP1+ TAMs spatially co-localize with hypoxic tumor borders`).
- **H4–H5 (Molecular Pathway & Upstream/Downstream Causal Axis)**: Identify the transcription factor, metabolite, kinase, or cytokine circuit (`Tumor-derived IL-11 drives STAT3-dependent TRAIL expression in astrocytes`).
- **H6 (Functional Perturbation / In Vivo Rescue)**: Prove causality via genetic KO/KD, conditional depletion, or pharmacological blockade (`Depletion of Spic+ macrophages impedes bone colonization`).
- **H7 (Human Clinical Validation / Therapeutic Synergy)**: Validate in patient specimens/cohorts and show synergy with standard-of-care/ICB (`PAI1 inhibition synergizes with anti-PD-1 and predicts survival in human PDAC`).

### 1.3 Top 30 Authentic Subheading Verbs (2025–2026)
`promotes` (#1), `reveals` (#2), `induces` (#3), `enhances` (#4), `drives` (#5), `identifies`, `increases`, `predicts`, `regulates`, `suppresses`, `correlates with`, `controls`, `inhibits`, `reduces`, `enables`, `impairs`, `improves`, `alters`, `mediates`, `activates`, `sensitizes`, `limits`, `reprograms`, `potentiates`, `remodels`, `exhibits`, `requires`, `rescues`, `abolishes`, `orchestrates`.

## 2. Five Non-Negotiable Results Prose Laws

1. **Inline Parenthetical Figure Citations (`765.3 : 1` Empirical Dominance)**:
   - Always make the **biological finding** the grammatical subject and cite the figure parenthetically at the end of the clause:
     - ✅ `Genetic ablation of Serpine1 markedly increased intratumoral CD8+ T cell infiltration (Fig. 3a,b and Extended Data Fig. 4c).`
     - ❌ `Fig. 3a shows that genetic ablation of Serpine1 increased CD8+ T cell infiltration.` *(Only `103` vs `78,831` inline citations in 2025–2026 CNS!)*
   - Exception: `As shown in Fig. 1a,` is acceptable *only* when introducing a complex schematic/experimental workflow diagram.
2. **Strict Past Tense for Observations (`4.93 : 1` Past : Present)**:
   - Use **Simple Past** for ALL experimental actions, observed patterns, statistical comparisons, and sequencing results (`we profiled`, `cells exhibited`, `expression was enriched`, `blockade reduced`).
   - Reserve **Simple Present** *only* for established biological truths or enduring conceptual statements in a closing sentence (`These findings indicate that X is required for Y`).
3. **The 3-Move Paragraph Anatomy**:
   - **Move 1 — Purpose / Rationale Hook (1 sentence)**: State *why* this next analysis/experiment was done (`To determine whether [X] directly drives [Y], we performed...`).
   - **Move 2 — Figure-Anchored Quantitative Findings (2–4 sentences)**: Report direction, magnitude, and statistical evidence (`...increased 2.8-fold (P = 0.002, two-sided Mann–Whitney test; Fig. 2c)`), covering the **5 statistical elements** where relevant (sample size `n`, summary statistic, comparison baseline, direction/magnitude, exact `P`/FDR).
   - **Move 3 — Objective One-Sentence Synthesis (1 sentence)**: Lock in the factual conclusion without drifting into Discussion speculation (`Together, these results demonstrate that [X] is essential for [Y] in vivo.`).
4. **Two Strict Taboos**:
   - **No Methods Repetition**: Mention the assay/model name in 3–6 words (`using multiplex immunofluorescence`, `via orthotopic implantation`) — NEVER recite buffer recipes, centrifuge speeds, antibody catalog numbers, or Seurat clustering parameters.
   - **No Deep Discussion**: Do not launch into 5-sentence literature reviews or broad clinical speculation inside Results; limit contextual notes to 1 brief clause (`Consistent with prior bulk analyses, ...`).
5. **Near-Ban on `data not shown` (`2.9%` in 2025–2026)**:
   - Modern CNS editors expect data in Extended Data / Supplementary Figures. Restrict `data not shown` strictly to trivial negative controls, baseline sanity checks, or confirmatory repeats where a panel adds zero visual value.

## 3. The 4-Tier Certainty Verb Ladder (Never Misuse or Overclaim Verbs!)

Derived from **`99,984`** verb-tier instances across `1,442` 2025–2026 Results sections. Match verb tier strictly to your **actual evidence level**:

| Tier | Evidence Standard Required | Permitted Authentic CNS Verbs (Ranked) | When Misusing Triggers Reviewer Rejection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Strong Causal / Genetic Proof** (`17,829`) | Conditional KO/CRISPR, genetic rescue, pharmacologic blockade *in vivo*, multi-model orthogonal validation | `revealed` (`6,556`), `demonstrated` (`3,246`), `confirmed` (`3,199`), `established` (`1,728`), `required` (`848`), `reversed` (`446`), `rescued` (`380`), `abolished` (`362`), `restored` (`349`), `abrogated` (`294`), `prevented` (`158`), `eliminated` (`120`), `drove` (`86`) | **NEVER** use `drove`, `established`, `required`, or `abolished` for pure scRNA-seq correlation, CellChat prediction, or retrospective survival curves! **Avoid `proved`** (`57` hits — virtually taboo in biology). |
| **Tier 2: Direct Empirical Observation** (`53,655`) | Differential expression, abundance shifts, spatial colocalization, flow/IHC quantification, *in vitro* assays | `showed` (`9,684`), `increased` (`9,517`), `enriched` (`6,144`), `identified` (`5,970`), `reduced` (`5,042`), `induced` (`4,679`), `mediated` (`3,061`), `enhanced` (`2,668`), `activated` (`2,260`), `regulated` (`1,512`), `suppressed` (`771`), `inhibited` (`768`), `impaired` (`641`), `promoted` (`516`) | Workhorse tier for factual reporting; pair directional verbs (`increased/reduced`) with quantitative fold-change or effect size whenever possible. |
| **Tier 3: Moderate Computational / Mechanistic Inference** (`9,565`) | Pseudotime/RNA velocity, ligand–receptor inference, pathway scoring, cross-cohort concordance | `consistent with` (`4,716`), `indicated` (`2,330`), `suggested` (`973`), `supported` (`641`), `highlighted` (`537`), `reflected` (`272`), `underscored` (`45`), `implied` (`38`), `pointed to` (`13`) | Mandatory when interpreting computational trajectories, CellChat/NicheNet interactions, or indirect spatial proximity prior to perturbation. |
| **Tier 4: Associative / Observational Caution** (`18,935`) | Cross-sectional patient cohorts, Kaplan–Meier / Cox regression, unperturbed spatial correlation | `associated with` (`6,496`), `may` (`4,165`), `could` (`3,387`), `correlated with` (`1,706`), `might` (`1,017`), `potentially` (`891`), `linked to` (`876`), `tended to` (`198`), `appeared to` (`174`) | Mandatory for retrospective TCGA/clinical correlations (`High X expression was associated with worse OS`, NEVER `"High X caused poor OS"`). |

## 4. High-Frequency Results Cohesion & Transition Corpus (`n = 1,442`)

### 4.1 Paragraph-Opening Rationale Hooks (`To [verb]...`)
Ranked by exact 2025–2026 CNS frequency:
- `To further [investigate / dissect / validate / elucidate] ...` (`448`)
- `To investigate whether [X] contributes to [Y], we ...` (`367`)
- `To determine whether [X] is required for [Y], we ...` (`275`)
- `To assess the clinical relevance / functional impact of [X], we ...` (`243`)
- `To evaluate whether [intervention] alters [phenotype], we ...` (`214`)
- `To identify upstream regulators / downstream effectors of [X], we ...` (`184`)
- `To test this hypothesis directly, we ...` (`158`)
- `To explore the spatial organization of [niche], we ...` (`152`)
- `To understand how [X] remodels [Y], we ...` (`122`)
- `To validate these findings in an independent cohort, we ...` (`121`)
- `To elucidate the underlying molecular mechanism, we ...` (`86`)

### 4.2 Inter-Paragraph & Intra-Paragraph Flow Bridges
- **Progression**: `We next examined/asked/performed...` (`1,780`), `Next, we...` (`1,354`), `Having established/shown that [X], we next sought to...` (`99`)
- **Emphasis / Surprise**: `Notably,` (`2,801`), `Interestingly,` (`1,162`), `Importantly,` (`819`), `Strikingly,` / `Remarkably,` *(use ≤1–2× per paper!)*
- **Addition**: `Furthermore,` (`2,090`), `Moreover,` (`1,780`), `In addition,`
- **Contrast**: `In contrast,` (`1,648`), `Conversely,` (`769`), `Whereas [A] exhibited..., [B] displayed...`
- **Concordance / Causal Bridge**: `Consistent with [previous panel/prior report],` (`1,434`), `Given that [X], we hypothesized that...` (`543`), `In line with these observations,` (`401`)
- **Paragraph Closing Synthesis (Move 3)**: `Together, these findings/results demonstrate/indicate that...` (`947` + `2,084`), `Collectively, these data...` (`672`), `Thus, ...` (`621`), `Overall, ...` (`472`), `Taken together, ...` (`409`)

## 5. Chinglish → Authentic CNS Results Rewrites

| Chinglish / Weak Draft (❌) | Authentic CNS Rewrite (✅) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `As can be seen from Figure 2A, the expression of SPP1 was obviously higher in tumor than normal.` | `SPP1 expression was markedly elevated in tumour relative to adjacent normal tissue (Fig. 2a).` |
| `Figure 3B shows that knockdown of X significantly inhibited cell proliferation.` | `Knockdown of X markedly impaired tumour cell proliferation (Fig. 3b).` |
| `We did GSEA analysis and got the result that EMT pathway was enriched.` | `Gene set enrichment analysis (GSEA) revealed significant enrichment of epithelial–mesenchymal transition (EMT) signatures (NES = 2.14, FDR = 0.003; Fig. 2d).` |
| `CellChat analysis proved that CAFs activate macrophages through FN1-CD44.` | `Cell–cell communication inference predicted enhanced FN1–CD44 signalling from CAFs to macrophages (Fig. 4c).` *(Tier 3, NOT Tier 1!)* |
| `There was no significant difference between the two groups (P = 0.42).` *(Acceptable, or upgrade to crisp biological subject)* | `Tumour burden did not differ significantly between control and treated cohorts (P = 0.42; Fig. 5e).` |
| `In order to verify our guess, we constructed knockout mice.` | `To test this hypothesis directly in vivo, we generated conditional X-knockout mice.` |
| `We found that high gene X is very related to bad prognosis.` | `High X expression was strongly associated with shortened overall survival (HR = 2.18, P = 0.001; Fig. 6a).` |

## 6. Results Internal Progression & Chaining Architecture (`n = 562` HTML Trees, `3,955` Subheadings)

How top-tier CNS authors propel Results forward like a cohesive **detective narrative** instead of disconnected figure captions:

### 6.1 Quantitative Module & Paragraph Blueprint (`n = 3,955` Subheadings)
- **Paragraphs per Results Subheading**: Median **`3` paragraphs** (IQR **`2–4`**, P10–P90 `1–5`).
- **Words per Results Subheading**: Median **`460` words** (IQR `310–651`).
- **Figure panel citations per paragraph**: Median **`3.0` panel calls** (IQR `2–5`, e.g., `Fig. 2a–c` + `Extended Data Fig. 2a`).
- **Subheading-End Synthesis Rate**: **`51.4%`** of all Results subsections close their final paragraph with an explicit factual lock-in (`Together / Collectively / Taken together / Thus, these findings...` `48.6%` + participial takeaway `2.8%`).

### 6.2 The Empirical "Given → New" Tail-to-Head Baton Pass (`2.9×` Continuity Law!)
Measuring biological-entity overlap between consecutive segments across `11,011` real pairs:
- Random paragraph pair in the same paper (baseline): **`6.6%`** overlap.
- **Intra-section chaining (`P_k` closing sentence → `P_{k+1}` opening sentence, `n = 7,618`)**: **`13.4%`** (**`2.0×` baseline**).
- **Inter-section chaining (`H_i` closing sentence → `H_{i+1}` opening sentence, `n = 3,393`)**: **`18.9%`** (**`2.9×` baseline!**).
- **Operational Rule**: Make the **new discovery at the tail of `P_k` / `H_i`** the **given premise at the head of `P_{k+1}` / `H_{i+1}`**.

### 6.3 Intra-Section Role Blueprint (`P1 → P2 → P3` Inside One Subheading)

| Paragraph Slot | Primary Rhetorical Job | Empirical Opening Shift (`P1` vs `P2+`) | Typical Authentic Openers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`P1` (Launch & Anchor)** | Hook onto the previous subheading's takeaway → pose this module's focal question → present primary Fig. X(a–c) readout | Higher **Purpose-Infinitive (`19.5%`)** and **Logical Premise Bridge (`5.2%` — `1.7×` P2+)** | `To elucidate how [X] activates [Y], we...`<br>`Given the marked differences in [X], we next compared...`<br>`The identification of [X] prompted the question of whether...` |
| **`P2` (Deepen / Contrast / Control)** | Drill down (pathway/spatial/subtype), test an alternative explanation, or run orthogonal validation / negative control | Sharp surge in **Sequential (`13.9%`)**, **Emphasis/Additive (`5×` P1)**, **Concordance (`3.7×` P1)**, and **Contrast/Paradox (`8×` P1)** | `We next evaluated whether...`<br>`Notably / Interestingly, ...`<br>`Consistent with these observations, ...`<br>`In contrast, ... did not significantly alter ...` |
| **`P3 / P_last` (Perturb / Validate & Lock In)** | Deliver decisive confirmation or cross-model check → **lock in the module takeaway (`51.4%`)** to hand off the baton to `H_{i+1}` | **Explicit Synthesis Close (`48.6%`)** | `Together, these data demonstrate that [X] is required for [Y].`<br>`Collectively, these results indicate that [X] acts via [Y], prompting us to examine [Z next].` |

### 6.4 Six High-Prestige CNS Inter-Section Chaining Patterns (With Verbatim Blueprints)

1. **Premise–Question Baton (`As / Given that [H_i finding], we next asked whether...`)**:
   - *Immunity* (H2 close → H3 open): `"...spatial organization of TAMs coincided with progressive vascular alterations."` ⟹ `"As vascular aberrancy would lead to hypoxia, we next asked if TAM patterning might be associated with the emergence of hypoxic zones."`
2. **Null / Paradox → Pivot Chain (`Unexpectedly no difference in A → Given the lack of difference in A, we evaluated B`)**:
   - *Cancer Cell* (H2 P1 close → P2 open): `"These data indicate that patients with HPD are unexpectedly not immune excluded."` ⟹ `"Given the lack of significant differences in the immune composition..., we then evaluated common oncogenic pathways."`
   - *Cell* (H1 P1 close → P2 open): `"CD8+ T cells were present at similar frequencies in both tissues (Fig. 1B)."` ⟹ `"We hypothesized that the functional states of CD8+ T cells might differ between the tumor and uiLN."`
3. **Deductive If–Then Prediction Bridge (`If [model], we would expect [testable signature]`)**:
   - *Cell* (H2 open): `"If anti-tumor CD8+ T cell responses are coordinated across tissues, we would expect clonally related CD8+ T cells to be present in both LN and tumor."`
4. **Discovery-Prompts-Question Bridge (`The identification of X prompted the question of whether...`)**:
   - *Cell* (H4 open): `"The identification of Tpex in uiLN prompted the question of whether and how Tpex and their surrounding cell neighbors respond to ICB in humans."`
5. **Cross-Section Back-Reference + Causal Escalation (`As [tissue] harbored [finding] (Fig. 2), we wondered whether [X] drives [Y]`)**:
   - *Cancer Cell* (H3 open): `"As melanoma from HPD patients harbored activated CD8+ T cells (Figure 2), we wondered whether CD8+ T cells unexpectedly support tumor progression in vivo."`
6. **Purpose-Infinitive Mechanism / Clinical Drill-Down (`To dissect how X activates Y...` / `To validate clinical relevance...`)**:
   - *Markov Stage Escalation (`n = 3,393` transitions)*: `Cohort/Atlas Discovery` → primarily feeds `Phenotype Characterization` (`50.2%`) & `Clinical/Spatial` (`22.5%`); `Molecular Mechanism` → directly escalates into `In Vivo Perturbation` (`16.5%`, highest cross-stage jump!) & `Clinical Therapy` (`11.0%`); `In Vivo Perturbation` → culminates in `Clinical Therapy / Translation` (`12.6%`).

### 6.5 Inline Citation Rhythm Inside Results (`Med = 4` Refs / Subheading; `n = 1,849` Subheadings)
- **Never write Results as a citation-free desert!** Across `256` lossless 2024–2026 CNS manuscripts, **Results is the #1 source of brand-new citations (`+27.0` NEW unique refs; `31.0` total unique; `17.0%` of all Results sentences; `85.3%` of subheadings carry `≥1` ref and `62.8%` carry `≥3`!)**.
- Naturally weave **`3–5` targeted primary citations per Results subheading** across the 7 empirical inline triggers (full breakdown in [09-citation-and-reference-architecture.md](./09-citation-and-reference-architecture.md) §2.3): (1) published gene signatures/markers/reference atlases (`14.4%`), (2) established mouse/organoid/spatial assays (`13.5%`), (3) one-clause concordance or contrast with prior work (`9.6%`), (4) clinical drug/biomarker benchmarks (`7.1%`), (5) mechanistic premise justifying the next step (`5.3%`), (6) named bioinformatics algorithms on first mention (`4.3%`), and (7) external validation cohorts (`3.1%`). Use explicit `[CITE: ...]` slots during drafting rather than fabricating citations.
