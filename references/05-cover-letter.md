# Cover Letter Writing Rules, Editorial Standards & Journal-Ready Templates

Synthesized from Elsevier Editor-in-Chief best practices and CNS editorial screening standards (built to win the **30-second editorial desk-triage**).

---

## 1. Seven Non-Negotiable Cover Letter Laws

1. **Strict 1-Page / `300–500` Word Ceiling**: Editors triage dozens of manuscripts daily in `< 60 seconds`. Exceeding 1 page or dense walls of text signals poor communication discipline.
2. **Never Paste or Paraphrase the Abstract**: The editor already has the Abstract one click away! Use the Cover Letter to answer **three strategic editorial questions** the Abstract does NOT explicitly pitch:
   - *What concrete bottleneck/paradox in the field does this solve?*
   - *Why is the advance substantial enough for THIS specific journal's broad/specialist readership?*
   - *How does it connect to and advance papers recently published in THIS journal?*
3. **Targeted Editor Salutation — Zero Generic Form Letters**: Address the specific Editor-in-Chief or handling senior editor by surname (`Dear Dr. [Surname],` or `Dear Editors of Nature Cancer,`). **NEVER** use `Dear Sir/Madam` or `To Whom It May Concern`, and double-check that the journal name is 100% accurate everywhere (wrong journal name = instant desk rejection).
4. **Zero Empty Superlatives or Hype**: Ban `groundbreaking`, `paradigm-shifting`, `revolutionary`, `first ever`, `unprecedented`, `world-leading`, `extremely significant`. Let concrete scope, methodological rigor, and causal/clinical impact speak with restrained authority.
5. **Explicit Journal Fit Citing 1–2 Recent Journal Articles**: Explicitly name why the manuscript matches the journal's scope and reference **1–2 directly related papers published in that journal within the past 1–3 years** (`Building upon recent work in Cancer Cell demonstrating [X] (Author et al., 2024)...`).
6. **All 4 Mandatory Editorial Compliance Declarations**: Every CNS / high-IF Cover Letter MUST cleanly include:
   - **Originality & Exclusivity**: Manuscript is original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere (note preprint DOI if posted on bioRxiv/medRxiv!).
   - **Authorship Approval**: All authors have reviewed and approved the final manuscript for submission.
   - **Competing Interests / COI**: Explicit disclosure (or declaration of no competing financial/non-financial interests).
   - **Ethics & Consent (when applicable)**: Institutional IRB/IACUC approval, Declaration of Helsinki compliance, written informed consent, and clinical trial registration ID if applicable.
7. **Strategic Reviewer Suggestions (Optional but High-Leverage)**: When permitted by the journal, suggest **3–5 independent, conflict-free domain experts** (Name, Affiliation, Email, 1-phrase specific expertise — never recent co-authors or same institution) and, only if strictly necessary, list up to 2 excluded reviewers with concise, professional conflict rationale.

## 2. The 5-Paragraph / 4-Module Architecture (`350–450` Words Optimal)

| Paragraph | Word Budget | Exact Purpose |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **P1 — Opening & Submission Metadata** | ~45–60w | Targeted salutation → Full manuscript title, article type (`Article`, `Resource`, `Technical Report`), and 1-sentence high-altitude positioning. |
| **P2 — Core Problem & Concrete Advance (NOT Mini-Abstract)** | ~110–140w | 1 sentence framing the urgent field gap/paradox → 2–3 razor-sharp sentences highlighting ONLY the flagship discovery, level of evidence (e.g., multi-cohort human spatial + *in vivo* genetic rescue), and why it shifts current understanding. |
| **P3 — Journal Scope Fit & Readership Appeal** | ~70–90w | Explain why this belongs in **[Target Journal]** specifically, linking to **1–2 recent publications** in that journal and naming the cross-disciplinary communities it will impact. |
| **P4 — Mandatory Editorial Compliance (+ Preprint / Companion Note)** | ~55–75w | Originality/exclusivity, all-author approval, COI statement, IRB/IACUC ethics compliance, funding/preprint disclosure if relevant. |
| **P5 — Reviewer Suggestions (Optional) & Professional Sign-Off** | ~40–60w | Concise reviewer note (or bullet list below signature) + restrained closing (`Thank you for your time and consideration...`) + Corresponding Author full name, title, institution, email. |

## 3. Good vs Bad Cover Letter Phrasing Table

| Weak / Desk-Reject Trigger (❌) | Authoritative CNS Editorial Phrasing (✅) |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Dear Sir or Madam,` | `Dear Dr. [Editor Surname],` / `Dear Editors of [Journal],` |
| `This is the first-ever groundbreaking study to solve cancer immunotherapy resistance.` | `Our work resolves a longstanding paradox in checkpoint blockade resistance by demonstrating that [X] locally orchestrates [Y] in vivo.` |
| `We performed scRNA-seq on 42 samples using 10x Genomics, clustered with Seurat, ran GSEA and CellChat, and validated with Western blot and IHC.` *(Methods log!)* | `By integrating single-cell and spatial multi-omics across 42 longitudinally sampled tumours with lineage-specific genetic ablation in mice, we establish a causal [X–Y] circuit driving [outcome].` |
| `We believe your journal is very famous and has a high impact factor, so we submit to you.` | `Given [Journal]'s leadership in publishing mechanistic studies of the tumour immune ecosystem—including recent work by [Author] et al. (2025) on [topic]—we believe our findings will be of broad interest to your readership.` |
| `We look forward to your positive decision and quick acceptance.` *(Pushy!)* | `Thank you for your time and consideration of our manuscript. We look forward to your editorial assessment.` |

## 4. Four Ready-to-Use Journal-Tailored Templates

### Template 1 — *Nature / Science* (Broad Conceptual Breakthrough, ~380w)
```text
[Date]

Dear Editors of Nature, [or Dear Dr. [Surname],]

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "[Concise 9–10 word Title]" for consideration as an Article in Nature.

[1 sentence: Broad conceptual paradox comprehensible across biology/medicine — e.g., Although immune evasion is a hallmark of solid tumours, whether therapeutic resistance reflects a global tumour property or is organized within spatially restricted microenvironmental niches remains a fundamental unanswered question.] Here, combining [high-level strategy: e.g., spatial functional genomics with multimodal patient profiling], we uncover [core biological principle — 2 crisp sentences: the exact causal mechanism + in vivo rescue/human relevance]. Importantly, [1 sentence: broader implication beyond a single cancer type or clinical/therapeutic shift].

We believe this study is ideally suited for Nature because it reveals a generalizable biological principle linking [Concept A] and [Concept B] that transcends [specific cancer type]. Our findings directly extend and conceptually advance recent discussions in Nature on [topic] (e.g., [Author et al., Nature 2024/2025]), and will appeal broadly to immunologists, cancer biologists, and translational clinicians.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not currently under consideration by any other journal [optional: A preliminary version has been posted on bioRxiv (doi: ...)]. All authors have reviewed and approved the final version of the manuscript. [All human tissue analyses and animal experiments were approved by the relevant institutional ethics committees (IRB/IACUC) with written informed consent obtained from all participants.] The authors declare [no competing financial interests / the competing interests disclosed in the manuscript].

We have listed suggested referees with relevant expertise in [field A] and [field B] in the online submission portal. Thank you for your time and consideration, and we look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,

[Corresponding Author Name], PhD / MD
[Title / Position]
[Department, Institution]
[Email Address]
```

### Template 2 — *Cell / Cancer Cell / Immunity* (Mechanistic Axis or Flagship Resource, ~400w)
```text
[Date]

Dear Dr. [Editor Surname] and Editors of Cancer Cell,

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled "[11–13 word Declarative Causal or Atlas Title]" for consideration as a Research Article [or Resource] in Cancer Cell.

[1 sentence: Specific oncology/immunology gap — e.g., While VEGFR-TKI plus anti-PD-1 combinations achieve high initial response rates in clear cell renal cell carcinoma, why long-term durability lags behind dual checkpoint blockade has remained mechanistically unresolved.] In this study, we leveraged [concise cohort/model scale] to uncover [flagship mechanistic discovery in 2–3 sentences, highlighting causal perturbation in vivo and validation across human clinical cohorts]. Together, these findings resolve [prior paradox] and nominate [specific actionable target/stratification strategy] to overcome treatment resistance.

Cancer Cell is the ideal venue for this work given its focus on mechanistic cancer biology with clear translational impact. Our study directly complements recent Cancer Cell articles examining [related niche/resistance theme] ([Author et al., Cancer Cell 2024]; [Author et al., Cancer Cell 2025]) by providing [exact incremental advance: causal perturbation / longitudinal spatial resolution / pan-cancer generality].

This manuscript represents original work that has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the submission and declare [no competing interests / competing interests detailed in the Declaration of Interests]. All human studies and animal procedures complied with institutional IRB and IACUC approvals.

Suggested reviewers with expertise in [Domain 1] and [Domain 2] (no conflicts of interest):
1. Dr. [Name], [Institution] ([email]) — Expertise in [specific topic].
2. Dr. [Name], [Institution] ([email]) — Expertise in [specific topic].
3. Dr. [Name], [Institution] ([email]) — Expertise in [specific topic].

Thank you for considering our manuscript. We look forward to your feedback.

Sincerely,
[Corresponding Author Block]
```

### Template 3 — *Nature Medicine / Cancer Discovery* (Clinical Trial, Biomarker or Translational Study)
- **Key Adaptation for P2 & P3**:
  - Replace aggressive mechanistic framing with **clinical utility, cohort scale (`n`), multi-center external validation, trial registration (`NCT...`), and actionable patient-selection impact**.
  - Explicitly state regulatory/ethics rigor (`prospective phase 1/2 trial registered at ClinicalTrials.gov under NCT...; CONSORT/TRIPOD compliant`).
  - Highlight why the assay/biomarker is **clinically deployable** (e.g., routine FFPE/H&E, cfDNA liquid biopsy, or standard IHC panel).

### Template 4 — *Nature Methods / Nature Biotechnology* (Computational Tool, AI Foundation Model or Assay)
- **Key Adaptation for P2 & P3**:
  - Frame P2 around the **specific technical trade-off** solved (e.g., *accuracy vs scalability*, *bridging routine H&E pathology with spatial transcriptomics*, *zero-shot cross-cohort generalization*).
  - Highlight **systematic benchmarking** (`benchmarked across X datasets and Y existing tools`), **demonstrated new biological discovery enabled by the tool**, and **open-source software/weights availability** (`publicly available at GitHub/Zenodo with reproducible tutorials`).
